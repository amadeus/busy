Install Busy

Busy keeps your Mac awake until its timer ends or its battery reaches your chosen cutoff. It then quits and allows normal sleep.

This installer adds Busy.app to Applications and the busy command to /usr/local/bin.

The next screen lets you choose the optional closed-lid helper. Select it to keep a laptop awake with the lid closed, including on battery. Without it, Busy prevents idle sleep while the lid is open.

The helper is authorized for the user currently logged in to this Mac. An existing helper keeps its authorized user and is updated automatically.

Quit Busy before continuing. Installation requires an administrator password. Busy will not launch automatically after installation.
